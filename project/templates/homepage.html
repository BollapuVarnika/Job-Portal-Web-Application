Our Role-Based Job Portal is a smart and secure online platform designed to connect job seekers with potential employers. The system uses role-based access control to ensure that each user — whether an Admin, Employer, or Job Seeker — can access only the features they need.
👥 Key Roles:
🔒 Admin:
      --Manages the platform, approves job listings, and oversees users and activities.
🏢 Employer:
     --Posts jobs, manages company profiles, and reviews applications from candidates.
🧑‍💼 Job Seeker:
     --Creates a profile, uploads a resume, searches for jobs, and applies with one click.
